OSCAR Docs

Open Source Copter for Academic Research (OSCAR) is a student-made, open-source drone platform designed for academic research and education. This documentation provides comprehensive guides, tutorials, and reference materials to help you get started with OSCAR and make the most of its capabilities.

About Us

Made by students,
for students.

Developed by a team of passionate students for their senior design project, OSCAR is designed to be accessible and adaptable for a wide range of applications, with open source documentation and resources to support your projects and learning journey. Whether you are a student, researcher, educator, or hobbyist, OSCAR provides the platform and resources you need to explore the world of drone technology.

The Development Team


Software Team:

The Software Team is responsible for developing the software components of the OSCAR drone customization platform. This includes the development and maintenance of the desktop application, which serves as the primary interface for users to configure the drone. It is designed for pre-flight configuration, post-flight observation, and inter-device communication validation. There does not currently exist free open-source drone hardware, so the intention of the desktop application is to provide a starting point for implementing custom drone implementations. Our application aims to be less complicated than existing solutions like Beta Flight, allowing users to implement their desired functionalities without the need to search for pre-existing applications. The team also works on this documentation website, ensuring that users have access to comprehensive guides and tutorials for using the platform effectively.

Firmware Team:

Bridging the gap between software, firmware, and hardware, the firmware team serves as the central integration point and final verification checkpoint for the entire stack. Responsibilities center on cross-functional alignment, system-level diagnostic testing, and maintaining technical continuity across complex, multi-layered projects.

Hardware Team:

The Hardware Team is responsible for the design, integration, and validation of the physical components of the OSCAR drone platform. This includes selecting and integrating components such as the flight controller, ESCs, motors, sensors, power systems, and communication hardware while ensuring compatibility across the entire system. The team also supports PCB and electrical design, wiring, hardware troubleshooting, and system-level testing to ensure the drone operates reliably and provides the necessary interfaces for firmware and software development. Documentation of the hardware architecture and configuration is maintained to support future customization and development of the platform.
